# Preliminary research/IP discussion — not legal advice

Potential implementation distinctions include explicit separation of learned STOP from answer authorization, source-bound acronym resolution and narrow policy validators. Their technical benefit must be measured independently; implementation effort is not patentable novelty. Prior-art areas include corrective/self-reflective RAG, adaptive retrieval and stopping, acronym disambiguation, rule-based evidence gates and structured policy QA. Stop-RAG and TASR are close stopping references; no exhaustive patent search or freedom-to-operate opinion was performed in this finalization.

The official VIT IPR and Technology Transfer policy (03-IPR-D001, amendment 16 April 2024, pp. 12–14) describes invention disclosure to the IPR & TT Cell and review involving approved attorneys. Verify the applicable campus/current policy with the university: https://vit.ac.in/files/IPR-and-Technology-Transfer-Policy.pdf and https://vit.ac.in/iprcell.

Recommended coordination: privately document implementation-specific claims and dated evidence; list all already-public repository/thesis/presentation disclosures; discuss them with the supervisor, VIT IPR office and a qualified patent professional before further disclosure. Ask counsel about novelty, inventive step, jurisdiction-specific software exclusions, inventorship/ownership, prior-art claim mapping and disclosure timing. Public code, conference papers and accessible theses can affect protection options; delaying or restricting disclosure also affects reproducibility and publication schedules. Seek a written university decision and then coordinate any filing and publication plan. This package asserts neither patentability nor non-patentability and offers no legal guarantee.

Conference readiness: suitable for an honest capstone demonstration and evidence-bounded draft. Not yet supported as a strong independent research submission: unapproved overlapping candidates, no independent human scores, a single small corpus, only four known development questions, no faithful external-method baseline and no monetary-cost finding. Resolve those gaps before a superiority/novelty claim. The proposed manuscript structure is abstract, problem, related work, method, experimental design, results, error analysis, limitations, reproducibility and verified references.

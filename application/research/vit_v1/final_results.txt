VIT HR SELF-CORRECTING RAG - FINAL RESULTS

Evaluation dataset:
23 VIT HR policy questions

Baseline:
Fixed-3 retrieval policy

Proposed method:
Learned halting policy

Results:
Fixed-3 average attempts: 1.957
Learned-policy average attempts: 1.565
Average attempts saved: 0.391 per question
Approximate retrieval-attempt reduction: 20%

Fixed-3 successful retrievals: 17/23
Learned-policy successful retrievals: 17/23

Fixed-3 success rate: 73.9%
Learned-policy success rate: 73.9%

Main finding:
The learned halting policy reduced the average number of retrieval
attempts while maintaining the same critic-defined retrieval success
rate on the 23-question VIT evaluation set.

Important limitation:
Success is based on the local critic threshold and has not yet been
fully validated using human answer-level ground truth.

Evaluation limitation:
The current evaluation contains 23 questions, so it should be
described as a pilot/small evaluation rather than a large benchmark.
